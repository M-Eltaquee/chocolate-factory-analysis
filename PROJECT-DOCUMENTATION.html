About This Project

A simple dashboard that shows how the chocolate business is doing — revenue, best-selling products, and how the sales team is performing. It has 4 pages, all built with the real sales numbers.

Every number shown across the 4 pages comes directly from the real sales data — nothing is made up or estimated.

The Look

Findings & Ideas
